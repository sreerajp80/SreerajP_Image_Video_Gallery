## 1. The issue

On the timeline screen, the "On This Day" strip and the timeline below it look almost the same:

- same square, rounded tiles on the same plain background,
- the only separators are the ✨ heading, the "1 year ago" captions and one thin grey line.

Because On This Day tiles do not support long press (on purpose), a user who cannot tell the two
areas apart thinks long press is broken. This happened while the Play screenshots were taken.

## 2. The fix (owner's design)

### 2.1 On This Day — border

- The whole On This Day section sits inside a rounded box (corner radius 16) with a thin border
  (1 dp) in the theme's `outlineVariant` colour, and 12 dp outer margin left and right.
- Theme colours only, so it looks right in light and dark mode.
- The thin `Divider` under the section is removed; the border does its job.

### 2.2 On This Day — shaded title bar

- The title row (✨ icon + "On This Day") gets a light background shade across the top of the
  box: `tertiaryContainer`, text and icon in `onTertiaryContainer`.
- Tertiary is a different colour family from the timeline headers (2.4), so the two titles do
  not look alike.

### 2.3 On This Day — collapse and expand

- The title bar is a button. Tap it to expand or collapse the section.
- **Collapsed by default.** Collapsed shows only the title bar, with:
  - a small count pill: the number of memory photos (for example "3"),
  - a down arrow (`expand_more`); expanded shows an up arrow (`expand_less`).
- Expanded shows the "1 year ago" rows of tiles, as today.
- The arrow has a localized tooltip; screen readers hear the title, the count, and whether it is
  expanded or collapsed (`Semantics(button: true, expanded: …)`).
- **Remembered for this app session only.** Every time the app starts, it is collapsed again.
  The open/closed state is held in a small Riverpod `StateProvider<bool>` (not saved to disk).
  This follows "collapsed by default" exactly. (If you would rather the app remember your last
  choice, it becomes one more field in `AppSettings`; say so and I will change the plan.)
- The "Show memories" switch in Appearance settings stays as it is: off still hides the section
  completely.

### 2.4 Timeline — shaded date headers

- Each timeline date header ("Today", "Yesterday", "3 November 2026", …) gets a light background
  shade: `surfaceContainerHigh` (a neutral grey-tint), with rounded corners (8) and the same side
  margin as the On This Day box.
- The header height stays exactly 48 dp (`kTimelineHeaderHeight`). The scroll scrubber and pinch
  zoom compute row positions from that number, so it must not change.

### 2.5 Scroll maths check

The timeline screen measures the On This Day height every time it needs it (`_leadingExtent`
reads the live render box). So when the section collapses or expands, the scrubber label and the
pinch anchor still land on the right row. I will test this after the change.
