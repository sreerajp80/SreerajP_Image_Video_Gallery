# Change log — "Memories" tab (On this day)

**Plan:** [plans/20261120_114942_memories-tab.md](../plans/20261120_114942_memories-tab.md)

## What changed

"On this day" moved from a collapsed row at the top of the Timeline to its own bottom-bar tab,
**Memories**, placed second: Timeline · Memories · Folders · Albums.

- The tab shows one section per earlier year, newest first, with **every** photo and video from
  today's date in that year. The old limits (5 years, 12 items per year) are gone.
- When today has nothing, it shows ±3 days around today in earlier years, with a note at the top.
  The window crosses month and year ends correctly (for example, on 30 December it reaches 2
  January). Matching uses calendar days only, so time zones and daylight saving cannot move a photo
  to another day.
- When even the week has nothing, a short empty message is shown. Pull-to-refresh works in all states.
- Tapping a tile opens the normal viewer. The grid uses the Timeline's column count.
- The Timeline row (`FlashbackCarousel`) and the "Show memories" switch in Settings → Appearance
  are removed. An old saved settings file or phone-move file that still has `showFlashbacks` loads
  fine; the value is ignored.

in both languages.

## Small differences from the plan

- The year header is two lines ("3 years ago", then the date) instead of one line joined with "·".
  This avoids a raw separator string in the widget.
- One extra key, `labelMemoryDateRange` ("{start} – {end}"), formats the week range. It holds only
  placeholders, so it was added to the "same as English allowed" list in
  `test/l10n/translation_parity_test.dart`, like `labelCompareBadge`.
- The help topic title "Timeline & Memories" was kept as it is.
