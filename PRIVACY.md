# Privacy Policy — Drishya Gallery

**Effective date:** 2 November 2026
**App:** Drishya Gallery (`in.sreerajp.imgvidgal`), for Android. Formerly named "SreerajP Image
Video Gallery"; only the name changed, not this policy.
**Contact:** sreerajp@zohomail.in

This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **the app collects
nothing, sends nothing to any server, and keeps your photos and videos on your phone.**

## 1. What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting
and no tracking of any kind. The developer receives no data from the app, ever.

## 2. What stays on your phone

To work, the app keeps some information **on your phone only**, in the app's private storage:

- **An index of your photos and videos** (names, dates, sizes, and details such as camera
  settings and location when the picture holds them), so the gallery can show and search them
  quickly. The pictures themselves stay where they are.
- **What you add:** albums, tags, favourites, notes, search history and settings.
- **The private vault:** pictures you move into it are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, under a key
  kept in your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore). Your vault PIN itself is never
  stored; only a salted hash of it is, in Android's protected storage.
- **Text read from a picture (OCR) and codes read from a picture** are kept only while you look
  at them, unless you choose to copy or save them.

None of this is copied to cloud backup or to another phone by Android's device-transfer feature:
the app turns both off.

## 3. Phone-to-phone transfer

The app can send pictures to **another phone you pair with by hand**, over your own Wi-Fi
network. This is the only time the app uses the network:

- It connects only to private, local network addresses (your home or office Wi-Fi), never to
  the internet.
- You start it on both phones, and pair by scanning a code or typing it.
- The connection is encrypted, and it stops when you leave the transfer screen.

## 4. Permissions

The app asks only for what it needs, and only when you use the feature:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Photos and videos | To show and organise your media |
| Camera | Only to scan the pairing code on the transfer screen. The app never takes photos itself |
| Fingerprint / face (biometric) | To unlock the vault, if you choose |
| Wi-Fi and network state, internet | Only for phone-to-phone transfer on your own Wi-Fi (Android needs the "internet" permission for any network connection, even a local one) |
| Change Wi-Fi state | Only to offer a Wi-Fi network from a scanned code to Android, which still asks you to confirm |

## 5. Sharing

The app shares a picture or text only when you tap **Share** and choose another app. What that
other app then does is governed by its own privacy policy. When you share a "clean" copy, the app
removes the location and camera details first; if it cannot, it does not share.

## 6. Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## 7. Deleting your information

Everything the app keeps is on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it, except pictures and
videos that you saved to your gallery, which belong to you. Items still in the vault are
encrypted with a key that is deleted with the app, so **restore them from the vault before
uninstalling**.
